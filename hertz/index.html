Hertz

Building the fleet operations and data platform under Hertz's managed rideshare fleets and autonomous vehicle partnerships.

Hertz runs managed rideshare fleets: cars Hertz owns and maintains, rented to rideshare drivers across multiple sites and geographies. That operation is also the foundation for autonomous fleets, where the “driver” is a partner’s software and questions of vehicle location, state, responsibility, and service get harder.

I lead product and data platform work for the division:

  • Fleet operations platform. Vehicle state management, maintenance workflows, and field tooling, architected as the operational layer for AV fleet management.
  • KPI infrastructure. Fleet tracking moved from spreadsheets and ad-hoc messages into structured data and reporting.
  • Workflow digitization. Out-of-service handling, vehicle and driver attribution, preventive maintenance, and vehicle turnover.
  • Driver performance dashboards. Prototypes surfacing behavioral and safety signals.

It’s the Revel problem at a company a few hundred times larger. The design goal: swapping a human driver for an autonomy partner should be a configuration change, not a rewrite.

Faster to build than to ticket

Not everything should wait for a roadmap slot. Routine fleet admin is slow by hand, one vehicle at a time: retiring a vehicle, renaming a batch after plate changes, putting cars on a preventive-maintenance schedule, clearing stale inspection defects, reconciling the insurer’s vehicle list, keeping the fleet card platform in step with Samsara.

So I automated it: ten small scripts, each doing one job, and a simple console to run them from.

It clears the backlog of small jobs now, and a working version is the best spec for the ones worth building into the platform.
